Shared-wallet model
One wallet, many operators. The org holds the wallet; people hold roles, not keys. Each user authenticates individually (passkey + MFA), acts within their role's permissions, and every action is attributed to the person — not the shared account. Keys never leave the MPC quorum: user share, org share, and the qualified custodian policy agent, which co-signs only inside policy.
Admin invites require a second admin's approval before activation — enforced by policy.

Separation of duties is enforced at the key layer: an initiator can never approve their own transaction, and no single admin can change policy alone.
Enforced by keys, not by UI. Every rule below is embedded in the signing policy held by the qualified custodian policy agent. A transaction outside policy doesn't get "blocked by the app" — it simply cannot be co-signed. Compliance isn't a setting; it's cryptography.

Dual approval over limit
Transfers above $50,000 require sign-off from 1 Approver in addition to the initiator. Above $250,000: 2 Approvers.
📇
Counterparty allow-list
External transfers only to 14 pre-approved addresses. New counterparties require Admin + Approver and a 24-hour activation delay.
📉
Per-role daily limits
Traders: $250,000/day across venues. Movers: $100,000/day external. Resets 00:00 UTC.
🏛
Venue restrictions
Trading wallet may only interact with Figure Markets, Hastra, and the predictions venue. All venues carry a Figure Risk Grade.
🔒
Yield Reserve withdraw-lock
Withdrawals from Yield Reserve require 2-of-3: Admin, Approver, and a 48-hour timelock. Deposits unrestricted.
🕑
Trading hours window
Trades execute 06:00–20:00 ET weekdays. Outside the window, orders queue for review.
Policy edits require dual-admin approval and take effect after a 24-hour delay. Every change is written to the audit trail.
